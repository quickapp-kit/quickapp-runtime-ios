### Boundary

The RPK Page IR and generated JS contain binding `6` for `Tabs.selected`, and the generated `onTabChange` assigns `this.selectedTab = event.index`. The iOS platform sends numeric `index` and the JS ABI accepts `double`. The remaining failure is after JS handler execution and before a RenderTransaction; it is not bypassed or changed in iOS. The likely boundary is shared JS event payload/handler state observation, which requires public JS/Core investigation.
